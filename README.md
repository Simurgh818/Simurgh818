# Hi there, I'm Sina Dabiri 👋

I am a Biomedical Engineering Ph.D. Candidate at the **Georgia Institute of Technology & Emory University** (Computational Neuroengineering Training Program Fellow) with a background spanning computational neuroscience, biological time-series analysis, and machine learning.

My research centers on decoding high-dimensional, noisy biosignals (scalp EEG, intracranial EEG, EMG) to isolate latent physiological dynamics, benchmark representation learning frameworks, and build reproducible scientific workflows.

---

### 🔬 What I Do

- 🧠 **Neural Population Dynamics & Decoding:** Applying linear (PCA, ICA) and non-linear (Autoencoders, latent-variable extraction) frameworks to isolate genuine physiological states from non-stationary noise.
- 📊 **Multimodal Biosignal Processing:** Developing robust preprocessing, artifact mitigation, and classification pipelines for complex physiological and imaging streams.
- ⚙️ **Scientific Computing:** Building modular, reproducible Python and MATLAB tools for neural data analysis and distributed high-performance computing (HPC) workflows.
- 🧪 **Cross-Disciplinary Translation:** Bridging computational models with human clinical studies, medical device V&V methodologies, and experimental neuroscience.
